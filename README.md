It's an attempt of making an F zero clone (Mostly of F zero X)
In order to play a game you need to first sign up and then log in with your new account.
Then you can race across 3 different race tracks (some tracks are bit glitched and didn't knew how to fix them)
After you finish a first lap you get access the boost which lets you trade life energy for extra speed.
After race finishes the score gets added the leaderboard.

## Warning: The LAN Multiplayer is very basic and was never made with security in mind so only attempt playing (if multiplayer will even work) it at your home LAN network.
The multiplayer is very glitchy and will probably brake the game after 1 race.

Some of my friends enjoyed playing this game and I hope that you will also enjoy trying it out.
